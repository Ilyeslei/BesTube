# Privacy Policy for BesTube

Last updated: July 2026

## Overview
BesTube is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed to operate locally on your device without collecting, storing, or transmitting any personal data.

## Data Collection and Usage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** BesTube does not collect, track, or share any personally identifiable information, browsing history, or user activity.
- **Local Storage:** Any user preferences (such as selected visual themes or accent colors) are saved locally within your browser using the standard extension storage API. This data never leaves your device.
- **Third-Party Services:** BesTube does not send data to external servers or third-party analytics services.

## Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue on the official GitHub repository.
